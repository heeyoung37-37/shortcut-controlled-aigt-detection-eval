The idea behind the benchmark

Less surface signal.
More to investigate.

Formatting, writing style, and word choice can offer easy clues to a detector. AIGT-SignalBench brings human and AI texts closer along those dimensions through controlled generation and realistic editing.

That makes room to study the signals that remain, including semantic-content flow. The controls reduce specific surface cues; they do not establish that every possible shortcut is removed.
